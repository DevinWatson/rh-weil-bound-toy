
## 6. Gaps, in order of dependence

1. S5 step 3 (the local structure of Q_i = FT(P_i)): containment Sing(Q_i) in Sigma_i; the form of Q_i at infinity as a sum over s of L_psi(h c_i s y) tensor E_s with E_s tame; tameness of Q_i at its finite singular points; propagation of this through every i <= k-1. I rederived the combinatorial recursion and the drop counts (section 1) and they agree with B, C, E. I did not and could not check the local-transform statements (Laumon 2.3.1 to 2.5.3, Katz-Gabber inside 2.5.3) that give tameness and the E_s; they are OCR-garbled in the dump, report D says "not verified beyond one step", and only report B has gone through it. Everything for k >= 5 hangs on it. What would settle it: a referee reading the page images of Laumon 2.3.3.1, 2.4.3, 2.5.3.1 and checking, with the invariants of S5, that (i) the slope-1 part at infinity of P_i is exactly sum of L_psi(a y) tensor tame, (ii) F^(0,inf') and F^(inf,0') send tame to tame, (iii) no slope > 1 part appears. A cheaper partial test (I did not run it, out of budget): exact fibre sums for d = 1 at k = 5 need q = 9 and about 28 field degrees, so no.
2. S5 steps 2, 4, 5 as cited statements: Laumon 1.3.2.3, 1.3.2.4, 1.4.2.1 (opened by B and D in OCR, garbled), BBD 4.3.1, SGA 1 V 8.2 (NOT OPENED). These are standard and I see no reason for them to fail, but they are not independently read. The meaning of "singular" (M' ramified, Phi_w != 0) must be fixed in the text.
3. Weight-freeness of the cited inputs: evidenced only by a grep of Laumon chapters 1 to 3 (report D) and by my memory for Katz-Laumon 2.1.3 and BBD chapter 4. If this matters for the claim "no weights", it should be checked by opening those statements and their proofs, not by grep.
4. S4 and S7 cited inputs (Kunneth, proper base change, projection formula, Leray, trace formula, Poincare duality on curves, Artin vanishing): standard, not opened by anyone including me, low risk. I rederived the shifts and signs and the k = 2 sign check is exact (section 1).
5. S3 counting and S10 limit: no longer gaps (refereed by me, PROVED). S11 duality: refereed, correct, uses Poincare duality (cited).
6. The Lean theorems: Parseval.lean and Endgame2.lean have no recorded main-session recompile; their statements match the use in S1, S2, S9. The fixed-field endgame mismatch (S10) is immaterial.

## 7. What I would do next
- Referee S5 step 3 from page images of Laumon, restricted to the three local statements named in gap 1.
- Try to produce an exact counterexample to S5 outside its hypothesis (G_k): the draft says the hypothesis is sufficient, not necessary; a clean example where (G_k) fails and the fibre cohomology still lives in degree k is already known (d = 1, q = 3, k = 3, c = (1,-1)); an example where it fails and cohomology appears in degree k+1 would show how sharp (G_k) is and would test the Sigma_i recursion directly. For d = 1 that needs k = 5 and q >= 9, which is out of reach by this method.
- If the user wants only the k -> infinity statement conditional on S5, state the theorem as "if S5 holds for infinitely many k then |alpha| <= sqrt q for odd squarefree d >= 3 over any odd q, and = sqrt q by duality"; everything else in the draft supports that statement.
