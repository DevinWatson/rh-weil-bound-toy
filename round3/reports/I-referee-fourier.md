What "slope" and "frequency" mean (OPENED, 2.1.2 as quoted at 1094 and 2.3.1.1): slope = upper-numbering break of an I_infinity-module; tame = all slopes 0 (wild inertia acts trivially). If an I_infinity-module has the form L_psi(a y) tensor T with T tame and a != 0, its Swan conductor is dim T and all slopes are 1; I call a its frequency. If a = 0 it is tame (slope 0). Two different frequencies never merge (L_psi((a_1 - a_2) y) has slope 1).

The type T3 check for P_i (hypothesis of 1.4.2.1(ii)): P_i = j_{!*}(M_i[1]) with M_i irreducible lisse. ## 2. The singular sets, the middle extension, the local structure

### 2.1 Singular set and drops, derived from proper base change and GOS (my derivation, independent of B's text)

Let P = j_{!*}(M[1]) be simple perverse of type T3 on A^1_{k-bar}, M lisse irreducible of rank r on U = A^1 - S, tame at every point of S, with I_infinity-module M_inf = T^0 + sum_a L_psi(a y) tensor T^a (T^0, T^a tame, a ranging over a finite set A of nonzero frequencies). Put m_s = dim M^{I_s}, a_s = r - m_s > 0 for s in S. Q = FT_b(P), b != 0.
- Stalk at w: Q_w = H^1_c(A^1, j_*M tensor L_psi(b w x)) provided H^0_c and H^2_c vanish. H^0_c = 0 (no compactly supported sections of a middle extension on the non-compact connected line). H^2_c is the coinvariants (twisted) of M tensor L_psi(b w x), zero because M has no subquotient L_psi(-b w x) (that is the T3 condition, for every w). PROVED modulo 1.3.2.3 and the T3 check.
- GOS: chi_c(A^1, j_*M tensor L_psi(bwx)) = r(1 - |S|) - Sw_inf(M tensor L_psi(b w y)) + sum_s m_s. With M_inf as above, Sw_inf = r - dim T^0 for w = 0 and Sw_inf(b w) = r - dim T^{a} (a = -b w) for w != 0, a in A, and = r otherwise. Hence generic rank r(Q) = sum_{s in S} a_s; drop at w != 0 is dim T^a if w = -a/b, a in A, and 0 otherwise; drop at 0 is dim T^0. PROVED (GOS cited, not opened there).
- Since Q = j_{!*}(M'[1]), Q is smooth at w iff its drop at w is 0 (2.2.1.1). So: Sing(Q) - {0} = {-a/b : a in A, T^a != 0}, and 0 in Sing(Q) iff T^0 != 0. This is exactly Laumon 2.3.1.2 in the reformulation I use. Equality, not just inclusion.
