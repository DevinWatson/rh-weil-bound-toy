## 1. Setting and what is claimed

p odd, q a power of p, l != p, psi_0 a nontrivial additive character of F_p, chi the quadratic character, f in F_q[x] squarefree of odd degree d >= 3 (d = 1 gives H = 0), Z the roots of f in F-bar, j: U_0 = A^1 - Z -> A^1, L = L_chi(f) the rank-one sheaf of the double cover y^2 = f(x) (A5 1.1: a character of pi_1(U_0) with values +-1, geometrically nontrivial, tame, trace of geometric Frobenius chi_n(f(x)), inertia acts by -1 at every root and, because d is odd, at infinity). H = H^1_c(A^1_{F-bar}, j_!L), Frobenius eigenvalues alpha_1..alpha_{d-1} (this dimension is by Grothendieck-Ogg-Shafarevich, C-9), with sum_{x in F_{q^n}} chi_n(f(x)) = -sum alpha_i^n.

Claim (Weil 1948). For every field isomorphism-embedding iota: Qbar_l -> C and every i, |iota(alpha_i)| = sqrt q.
The proof proves |iota(alpha_i)| <= sqrt q (S0 to S10) and then equality (S11).

## 4. The proof, step by step

Hypothesis set used throughout (fixed once): p odd; f squarefree of odd degree d >= 3 over F_q; k >= 2.

### S0. Reductions (A5 section 3; PROVED)
Statement. Let s >= 1, Q = q^s > d. There is a in F_Q with f(a) != 0. With f_a(x) = f(x + a): f_a is squarefree of degree d, same leading coefficient, f_a(0) != 0, roots Z - a; the geometric Frobenius over F_Q acts on H_a = H^1_c(A^1_{F-bar}, j_!L_{chi_Q}(f_a)) with eigenvalues alpha_i^s (multiplicity kept), H_a = H as F_Q-Frobenius module; any bound |beta|^k <= Q^{(k+3)/2} for the eigenvalues beta of H_a gives |alpha_i|^{sk} <= q^{s(k+3)/2} and, taking the exact positive s-th root, |alpha_i| <= q^{1/2 + 3/(2k)}.
Argument. Base change and translation by the F_Q-automorphism x -> x + a, which carries L_chi(f) to L_chi(f_a). Dimension and vanishing H^0_c = H^2_c = 0: H^0_c by a direct argument, H^2_c = coinvariants(-1) of a nontrivial rank-one L (C-5), dim H = d - 1 by C-9. Order of choices (the draft omitted it; A5): Q first with Q - 1 > (k^2/2 + k) d^2 (draft's constant; A4's sharper Q - 1 > (k^2/4) d^2 is also PROVED but I keep the cruder one), then a, then replace f by f_a, then c by S3 for the root set Z - a. The set of root ratios depends on a. Example: f = x^3 - x over F_3 vanishes at every point of F_3, so F_9 is needed. CHECKED: A5 `compute/A5/out_more.txt` (translations in F_9, 20 translations agree, base change D_3(u)D_3(-u) = D_9(u^2)).
Status: PROVED mod C-5, C-9. Cosmetic corrections to the draft: target inequality is |alpha|^{sk} <= q^{s(k+3)/2}, not "|alpha|^s <= q^{s/2}-type".

### S1. The elementary identity and the fibre bound (CHECKED in Lean; PROVED)
For every finite field F, nontrivial additive character psi of F, t: F -> C with |t| <= 1, k >= 2, c_1..c_{k-1} in F^*, Q_c(x) = sum c_i x_i x_{i+1}, S_h = sum_x prod t(x_i) psi(h Q_c(x)), R = sum_{h != 0} S_h, N = sum over {Q_c = 0} of prod t(x_i), M = sum t:
(a) |F| N = M^k + R, with no hypothesis on c (zero coefficients allowed);
(b) for h != 0: |S_h| <= |F|^{(k-1)/2} sum |t|^2 <= |F|^{(k+1)/2}; hence |R| <= (|F| - 1)|F|^{(k+1)/2} < |F|^{(k+3)/2}.
Argument: orthogonality of psi; k - 1 Parseval steps along the path (the matrices B_i = (psi(h c_i x y)) satisfy B_i B_i^* = |F| Id, operator norm |F|^{1/2}; A4 3.1 gives the same proof on paper).
Lean (A7, Lean 4.34.1, exit 0, no sorry, axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound only; logs `compute/A7/ToyWeil.Weighted.log`, `ToyWeil.Unbounded.log`): `Weighted.lean` theorems `identityW`, `iterated_boundW`, `identity_bound_oneW` (the draft's earlier Lean files `Identity.lean`, `Parseval.lean` covered only c_i = 1; this repairs that). `identity_bound_oneW` states |q N - M^(n+1)| <= (q - 1) q sqrt(q)^n for n >= 1, which is the bound on R with the exponent bookkeeping of the draft. The main session recompiled Identity.lean in round two; I have no record that it recompiled Weighted.lean, but A7 reports the compile.
Also CHECKED exactly (A4, `compute/A4/check_S1_out.txt`): 77 of 77 cases (f cubic or linear over F_3, n up to 4, k = 2, 3, 4) satisfy the identity and the bound; the bound is attained for k = 3.
Not in Lean: that psi o Tr is a nontrivial character of F_{Q^n} (trace surjectivity; standard).

### S3. Choice of c (PROVED; counting is A4's and the draft's, refereed by A4 and A5 and checked)
Fix k >= 2, Q = q^s, and (after S0) f with f(0) != 0. R_ratio = {z'/z : z, z' in Z} (it contains 1; at most d^2 elements). rho_i = -c_{i-1}/c_i (2 <= i <= k-1). Sigma_1 = {0}, Sigma_i = rho_i (Z union Sigma_{i-2}) (Sigma_0 = empty), closed form Sigma_i = {rho_i rho_{i-2} ... rho_m z : z in Z, m = i, i-2, ..., >= 2} union ({0} if i is odd). Condition (G_k): Z disjoint from Sigma_i for 1 <= i <= k-1 (i = 1 is f(0) != 0). Equivalently every product rho_i rho_{i-2} ... rho_m that occurs is outside R_ratio. h does not occur.
Existence: each excluded condition is "a Laurent monomial in the c_i equals a given r in R_ratio"; the monomial has c_i (the largest index in it) with exponent -1, so for fixed other coordinates it excludes at most one value of c_i, hence at most (Q-1)^{k-2} points of (F_Q^*)^{k-1}. At most (k^2/2 + k) d^2 conditions (draft), at most (k^2/4) d^2 (A4). So c exists once Q - 1 exceeds the count. CHECKED: A4 `compute/A4/check_S3_out.txt`: union bound holds in 5 of 5 runs; A1 `bookkeeping_output.txt` and A3 `closed_form_check.py`: the closed form equals the recursion for i <= 8 (A1, A2) and i <= 14 (A3). All-ones quadric fails (G_k) from k = 5 (rho_4 rho_2 = 1); first failure of the all-ones quadric is at i = 4 in A1's indexing, i.e. Sigma_4 meets Z, which concerns the passage to P_5, i.e. k = 5. c_1 = -c_2 fails at i = 2.
Consequence (A4 4.2 (iv)): s(k) must grow with k, at least like log_q(k^2 d^2).
Caveat (A3): for d = 2 with both roots in F_4, R_ratio is all of F_4^*, so (G_3) fails for every c; the field must be chosen after the roots, which the order of choices in S0 does.

### S8. Fibre bound on the constant quotient (A4 3.3; PROVED given S1, S2, S6)

### S11. The lower bound by duality (A5 section 2; PROVED mod C-1, C-5, C-9, C-12)

Argument.
